Filters and driver help

You choose who drives you. Clarity is not taking bookings yet; this is how it is planned to work.

See your choices on the map

The map shows the closest 6 drivers who are free straight away, any who are finishing a single trip, and whether one of your favourite drivers is available.

Specialised filter lists

  • Favourite drivers. Pick from the drivers you already know and trust.
  • Female drivers only. For women and children who would feel more comfortable.
  • Language. Choose a driver who speaks the language you want.

All of it is visible in the app, so you can see who is available before you book.

Pet-friendly and wheelchair-accessible cars

Filter for pet-friendly and wheelchair-accessible cars, so the right car comes the first time. Availability depends on our drivers and their vehicles. Dedicated pet and wheelchair-accessible cabs, with the whole car set up for the purpose, are coming soon.

Never late for pickup

For the hard of hearing, and as an early alert for everyone, we have built an alarm you cannot miss when your driver is close.
