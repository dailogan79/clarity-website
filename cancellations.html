Cancellations and refunds

Plans change. Here is what happens if you cancel, and what to do if something went wrong.

Clarity is not taking bookings yet. This page describes how it will work when we launch.

If you cancel before your driver arrives

Cancelling is free until your driver sets off to you, and for the first 30 seconds of their journey to you. After that the fee goes up the longer they have been on the way and the further they have driven. It steps up as soon as either the time or the distance is reached, whichever comes first.

The fee is never more than £4.00 while your driver is still on the way. It all goes to your driver for the time and fuel spent. Clarity takes no share. The small extra over what your driver receives covers the card company's fee. The app always shows the exact fee before you confirm.

If your driver has arrived

  • You have 90 seconds free to come out. After that, waiting is charged by the second.
  • If you cancel once your driver is at your pick up, you pay £4.00 (the £3.50 fee plus the card fee), plus any waiting time already built up.
  • If you do not come out, the driver can cancel after about 4 minutes. It costs the same as cancelling: £4.00 plus the waiting time.
  • You can buy up to 3 minutes of extra waiting at your pick up. Your app offers this before the driver is able to leave.

If you end a trip once it has started

You pay the larger of 80% of the fare, or the share of the journey already travelled, plus any extras already built up, such as waiting. Your driver finds a safe place to stop.

Times you pay nothing

  • Your driver cancels, or no driver can be found.
  • Your driver is still finishing another trip. The clock only starts once they head to you.
  • A driver fails the identity check at the start of your trip. The trip ends and you pay nothing.

Any hold on your card is released, usually within a few days depending on your bank.

If something went wrong with a trip

  • Tell us within 14 days using the app or by email: admin@claritycabs.co.uk.
  • A member of our office team reviews every complaint. We may ask for details.
  • Where money is due back, it is refunded to the card you paid with. Your bank can take a few days to show it.
  • If a driver fails the identity check at the start of a trip, the trip ends and you pay nothing.

Your rights

This page does not take away any rights you have under UK consumer law.
