Family, and booking for someone else

Clarity is not taking bookings yet; this is how it is planned to work. It is for parents, for people who look after someone, and for anyone who books a taxi for a friend or relative.

Had a text from a parent?

Your parent has set up a Clarity account for you. When the app is in the stores (Google Play and the App Store, coming soon):

  1. Install the Clarity app.
  2. Sign up with the mobile number your parent used. It is the number the text message was sent to.
  3. That is all. Your account is linked to your parent's as soon as you sign in.

The text works for 24 hours. If it runs out, ask your parent to send it again.

For parents and guardians

  • Add your child (aged 13 to 15) in your Profile, under Linked people. We text them how to get started. Clarity is not available to children under 13.
  • You approve every ride they ask for. Nothing is sent to a driver until you say yes, and you pay for it. If nobody answers in time, the ride is cancelled and nothing is charged.
  • Up to 4 parents or guardians can be linked to one child. They are all asked, and the first to answer decides.
  • A child with no phone can still be linked: you book for them.
  • At 16 the account becomes their own, and they choose whether to stay linked to the family.
  • Planned: following a child's trip live on your own phone, and being told when they are picked up and dropped off.

Looking after an adult

  • Link up to 20 people you look after or help. They have their own Clarity account and have to say yes.
  • When you book for them, you choose who pays. If they are to pay, we ask them to confirm first. If they have no card, or do not answer, you pay.
  • Whoever pays gets the receipt.

Booking a taxi for a friend or relative

  • They have the Clarity app. We ask them to share where they are, for this booking only, and they have to say yes. The driver, the car and the PIN show in their own app.
  • They do not have the app. They get a text message with the driver, the car and the PIN. The driver only ever sees their first name.
  • You can watch the taxi come on your own phone, as you would for your own ride. They never see what you paid.
  • Planned: a link for people without the app to follow the ride, and their own emergency button on a trip you booked for them.

Clarity remembers the people you book for, and what you chose last time, so a repeat booking is quicker. You can delete them at any time.
