Your parent has set up a Clarity account for you

Clarity is not in the app stores yet. When it is, this is how you get started.

Getting started

  1. Install the Clarity app. It is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.
  2. Sign up with the mobile number your parent used. It is the number the text message was sent to.
  3. That is all. Your account is linked to your parent's as soon as you sign in.

Good to know

  • Child accounts are for ages 13 to 15. Clarity is not available to children under 13.
  • Your parent approves every ride you ask for, and they pay for it.
  • The text message works for 24 hours. If it runs out, ask your parent to send it again.
