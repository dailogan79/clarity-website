How we operate

Clarity operates with the intention to be utterly clear and fair to both our drivers and our customers. Every trip has a breakdown for the passenger and for the driver, and any changes will be announced, so there are no surprises.

We won't charge you more because we got busy and there are not enough cabs in the area, and there are no "surge" charges, mid-trip or otherwise. We charge for the distance and time a journey should take, so you know the estimated or fixed price up front, and so does your driver. See our For passengers and For drivers pages for the complete breakdowns.

We have also built in features we are proud of, like alarms that actually ring when your cab is close (not just a notification), female-only drivers for women and children, favourite drivers, and language, pet-friendly and wheelchair-accessible filters, so your journey is personalised to your preferences. (Availability depends on our drivers and their vehicles.)

The best bit of Clarity: if there is ever an issue, you can call our Swansea office and speak to a local person, whether it is a problem with a driver, lost property, or you are struggling to book a cab in an area that has none.
