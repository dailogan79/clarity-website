Passenger safety

Safety is built into every trip, before, during and after. Clarity is not taking bookings yet; this is how it is planned to work.

Before your driver arrives

  • Checked drivers. Licence, badge, insurance and car paperwork are checked, and every driver meets the Clarity office in person, before they start.
  • Photo checks. Drivers take a selfie before they work, and you can see your driver's photo, car and number plate in the app.
  • An alarm you cannot miss when your driver is close, so you are ready and never rushed.

When your driver arrives

  • Is this your driver? If the person, car or plate is not what the app showed, tell the app. Our safety features stop that driver from working and we book you another taxi straight away.
  • Your PIN. You give it to your driver to confirm you are both who you should be, and only then does the trip start.

During the trip

  • A panic button in every trip. It alerts the Clarity office, and can call 999 for you.
  • Share your ride. Choose people you trust to follow your trip live.
  • An emergency contact set up before your first ride.
  • Change your mind safely. Depending on your fare you can add stops, change stops or end the trip early.

After the trip

  • A full receipt that breaks down every penny, kept for two years.
  • Rate your driver, and report anything that worried you. A local person at the office follows it up. Contact us
