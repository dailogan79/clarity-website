Incen-tip™: a fair deal on a long pickup

Our Incen-tip™ is one of the things we are proudest of. Every Clarity fare includes a pickup fee that covers the short distance and time it takes a driver to reach you. But sometimes the nearest driver is a long way off, for example out on the Gower, and a long drive to get to you would normally go unpaid.

We do it differently. When that happens, the app shows you how far away your driver is and how long they would take, and our Incen-tip™ calculator works out exactly what we would charge to take someone for that extra leg of the journey. It suggests that amount as an optional tip, and you decide. Add it, keep waiting, or call our local office.

It means you get a cab when you need one and nobody lets you down, and it is there to make sure both passengers and drivers are treated fairly.

An Incen-tip is a tip, so it goes to your driver. Your driver keeps 98.5% of it: the 1.5% card fee comes out of the tip, and Clarity takes nothing.

How we put the Incen-tip™ together

How it came about: it started with a real night. After a wedding down on the Gower, our founder, David, was stranded and could not get a cab home. The drivers did not want to travel from Swansea to Rhossili Bay for the fare they were offered, and the app had no way to add extra money to get home. The thought was simple: if only I could have reached the driver before the job was turned down, I would have gladly paid more than the fare on offer, and the driver might have said yes.

That became the Incen-tip™, short for incentivised tip: a tip offered before the driver arrives, to make the longer journey worth it. It helps the passenger get home, and it helps the driver, because it is a tip they keep for themselves, and that means the world to them. Then we built a calculator so the amount is worked out properly, not guessed.

Here is the sum, so you can see exactly why it exists and where the number comes from.

  1. 1
    We measure the real drive. The app uses the real road distance and driving time from your nearest driver to you, not a straight line.
  2. 2
    We price that drive like a fare. Miles and minutes only, at the normal rate for the time of day, with no free first mile, because the driver really does use that fuel and time.
  3. 3
    We take off the pickup fee (£3.50), which is already in your fare.
  4. 4
    We take off Clarity's share (15%), so you are not asked to pay anything for that drive that we would not take on any other fare.
  5. 5
    We round it to the nearest 25p. That is the Incen-tip™ we suggest.

First, getting set up

A few minutes of setup once, so every trip after it goes smoothly and safely.

  1. 1
    Sign up. Your name, your mobile number and a text code to prove it is you. That is all you need to get started.
  2. 2
    Build your profile, with a photo of your face. It is a small step that makes a big difference. Your driver can see who they are picking up, and you can see who is coming. Our drivers are checked with a photo too, so you both know who is who.
  3. 3
    Choose your pickup options at the start. Tell us how you get around, and about any pets or assistance you travel with. We use it to make sure you always get the right cab, first time, not a car that cannot take you.
  4. 4
    Add your card, protected by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe, one of the world's biggest payment companies, and we never see or store them. Only your fare and our 35p booking fee are taken, when the trip ends, and your receipt shows every penny.

Then, every trip

Set up once, then each trip goes like this.

  1. 1
    Say where you are going. You see the price before you book. The map is clear at a glance: the closest 6 drivers who are free right now, drivers finishing a single trip, and your favourite drivers if they are available.
  2. 2
    Choose who drives you with the filters, or open the trip to everyone.
  3. 3
    Be ready on time. The alarm sounds when your driver is close.
  4. 4
    Check who has arrived. Photo, car and plate are in the app. Not who you expected? Tell the app and we deal with it.
  5. 5
    Share your PIN. It proves you are both who you should be, and starts the trip.
  6. 6
    Stay in control. Depending on your fare, add stops, change stops or end the trip early.
  7. 7
    Rate your driver, and tip if you would like to. Do it now, before your payment is processed. That saves you extra card fees, because a tip added later is a separate card payment with its own fee. Your driver keeps 98.5% of every tip: the 1.5% card fee comes out of the tip, so it costs you nothing extra.
  8. 8
    Get your receipt, broken down to the penny.

A 35p booking fee on every trip

It covers what it costs us to run each trip: card payments, maps and servers. We don't make money from it. It's shown as its own line on your receipt, and it is on top of the driver's fare, so the driver is paid the same.
