A flat 15%, and 98.5% of every tip

Money you can check

Incen-tip™: paid fairly for a long pickup

Our Incen-tip™ is one of the things we are proudest of. The pickup fee passengers pay covers the short distance and time it takes you to reach them. But a long drive to a pickup would normally go unpaid.

We do it differently. When the nearest driver is a long way from a passenger, our Incen-tip™ calculator works out what we would charge to take someone for that extra leg of the journey, and the app suggests it to the passenger as an optional tip. The passenger decides, and it is there to make sure passengers and drivers are both treated fairly.

How it came about: our founder, David, was stranded down the Gower after a wedding. Drivers did not want to come from Swansea to Rhossili Bay for the fare on offer, and the app gave our founder no way to add more. David would gladly have paid extra to get home. So the Incen-tip™ (short for incentivised tip) lets a passenger offer a tip before the driver arrives, a tip the driver keeps for themselves, and our calculator works out a fair amount.

See the sums and worked examples on the passengers page.

It is a tip, so it is yours: you keep 98.5% of it, after the 1.5% card fee, and Clarity takes nothing.

First, getting set up

We check everything properly, so passengers can trust every Clarity driver and you can trust the company you work for.

  1. 1
    Sign up in the app. Your details, your car and a text code to confirm your number.
  2. 2
    Get your documents ready. You will need:
    • Your photo driving licence
    • Your private hire driver badge
    • Your vehicle (plate) licence from the council
    • Insurance for your car
    • A current MOT certificate
    • Your DBS check, and a background check
    • Your V5C (the vehicle logbook)
    • Photos of your car
  3. 3
    Come in for an interview. You meet the Clarity office in person and bring your original documents. We go through them with you, and it is a chance to ask us anything.
  4. 4
    We put everything together. The office checks and approves each document, and helps you set up your payouts so your earnings reach your bank account.
  5. 5
    Training. We show you how to use the app and how we operate, before you take your first job.
  6. 6
    Terms and conditions. Read and agree to them before you start. Read the terms
  7. 7
    Still not sure? Check your earnings with our calculator. What you earn depends on how busy we are and how quickly we can grow. Go to the calculator
