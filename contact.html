Contact us

Riders, drivers and anyone with a question: the Clarity office is here to help.

Email

admin@claritycabs.co.uk

We aim to respond to any email within 48 hours, but this may depend on how busy we are and how urgent the email is.

Our Swansea office

Clarity will have a local office in Swansea. If you have a problem with a trip, you will be able to call the office and speak to a local person. The phone number will be added here before we launch.

Opening times

Booking: once we launch, the app will be open 24 hours a day.
Our office: open 8am to 10pm every day. We will extend this as Clarity grows.

Safety

In an emergency always call 999. During a trip, use the red button in the app. It tells our office and, if you wish, the person you have chosen.

Drivers

Interested in driving for Clarity? Email us with your name and your Swansea licence details and we will be in touch.
