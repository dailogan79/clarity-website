Privacy: summary

The full Privacy Notice is shown in the app and is being finalised with our solicitor. Here is a plain summary.

This website

This website collects no personal information. It has no forms, no accounts and no cookies. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service, to count how many people visit each page (which page, the country, the type of device and where the visit came from). It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other websites, and is designed so that individual visitors cannot be identified. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply. Like any web host, ours may keep basic technical records, such as visitors' internet addresses, for security.

What we use, and why

  • Your name, email and mobile number to create your account, confirm it is you, and send receipts and codes.
  • Your location during a booking, so a driver can find you and you can follow your trip.
  • Your photo, if you add one, so your driver can recognise you. It is taken on your phone and sent only for the trip. It is removed from the driver's app once you have been collected.
  • Trip records and receipts for two years, so you can see them and so we can deal with complaints.
  • Safety information: trips may be recorded, and an emergency contact you choose is told only if you ask us to.

Your card

Your card details go straight to our payment provider, Stripe. Clarity never sees or stores your card number, expiry date or security code. We keep only a reference, the card brand and the last four digits.

Who sees your information

  • Your driver sees your first name, your photo (if you added one) and your pick up and drop off, and only after they accept the trip.
  • Our office team sees what they need to look after your account and help you.
  • Our service providers, such as payments and maps, see only what they need to do their job. We do not sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and to object to how we use it. Clarity Cabs UK is responsible for your information. Email admin@claritycabs.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

This summary is not the full notice. If it differs from the full Privacy Notice in the app, the full notice applies.
