Terms of use: summary

The full terms are shown in the app when you sign up and are being finalised with our solicitor. This is a plain summary of the main points.

Who we are

Clarity will be a booking service that connects riders with licensed taxi drivers in Swansea. We are not taking bookings yet. It is run by Clarity Cabs UK, 9 Tawe View Crescent, Morriston, Swansea SA6 6HD.

Your account

  • You must give true details and keep your login private.
  • Please set up your emergency contact, your photo and your payment card before your first trip.
  • We may suspend an account that is used unsafely or unfairly.

Trips and payment

  • Clarity is card only. See Fares and Cancellations and refunds.
  • A hold is placed on your card when you book. You pay the real fare at the end.
  • If a payment fails, the amount owed to Clarity must be settled before your next trip.

Safety

  • Trips may be recorded for everyone's safety.
  • Please treat drivers with respect. Drivers may end a trip, safely, if they feel unsafe.

This summary is not the full contract. If it differs from the full terms in the app, the full terms apply. Nothing here limits your legal rights as a consumer.
