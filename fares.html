Fares and fees

You always see the price before you book. Here is how it is worked out.

Clarity is not taking bookings yet. This page describes how it will work when we launch.

An example: a £20.00 fare

You pay£20.00. No card fee is added on top.
Your driver receives£17.00 (85%)
Clarity receives£3.00 (15%)
Card company's feeAbout £0.50 (1.5% plus 20p), paid by Clarity out of its £3.00, so Clarity keeps about £2.50

If you add a £2.00 tip, your driver keeps 98.5% of it, because the 1.5% card fee for processing it comes out of the tip. Clarity takes nothing from a tip. Your receipt in the app always shows the real split for your trip.

Two ways to pay for a trip

Fixed fareThe price is held whatever the traffic or route. The only extra is waiting, if you keep the driver waiting.
Variable fareThe price you are quoted is the fare you pay. It only goes up for time over the allowance, a route change you agree to, or a stop you add during the trip. The app shows the cost before anything is added.

Every fare is worked out on the quickest route, with a few minutes added for getting in and out of the car. A group of five or more has one fixed increase of 20 per cent. A wheelchair user never pays extra.

Waiting

  • At your pick up you have 90 seconds free to get in. After that, waiting is charged by the second.
  • Waiting at a stop you added is charged from the first second.
  • Nothing is charged for waiting at the drop off, on any fare.

Paying

  • Card only. There is no cash and no split fares.
  • When you book, a hold is placed on your card for the estimated fare plus a safety margin, so we know the fare can be paid. You only pay the real fare. The rest is released, usually within a few days depending on your bank.
  • Your card is kept by our payment provider, Stripe. Clarity never sees or stores your card number.
  • Your receipt shows the pick up, every stop, the drop off and how the fare was made up. It is kept in the app for two years.

Tips

A tip is entirely optional. Your driver keeps 98.5% of it; the 1.5% card fee for processing it comes out of the tip. Clarity takes nothing from a tip. The best time to tip is straight after you rate your driver, before your payment is processed: that saves you extra card fees, because a tip added later is a separate card payment with its own fee.

Where the money goes

Clarity keeps a flat 15% of the trip fare. The card company's fee on the trip fare is paid out of that share, so it is never added on top for you. The rest goes to your driver.

Prices and rates can change. The price you are shown when you book is the one that applies to that trip.
